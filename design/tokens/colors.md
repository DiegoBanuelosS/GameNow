## Color tokens

Semantic roles for GameNow. Palette: black canvas, bone text, one ember accent.

- `color-bg-canvas`: `#000000` – page background
- `color-bg-surface`: `#1a1815` – inputs and grouped controls
- `color-bg-subtle`: `#221f1b` – hover / pressed surfaces
- `color-fg-default`: `#ece7de` – primary text
- `color-fg-muted`: `#9c9588` – labels, helper text, secondary actions
- `color-accent-primary`: `#f2622e` – primary actions
- `color-accent-on-primary`: `#1a120e` – text on the accent
- `color-border-subtle`: `#2e2b26` – input borders
- `color-border-strong`: `#4a453d` – hover borders
- `color-border-critical`: `#d97a74` – errors
- `color-fg-critical`: `#f0b4af` – error text
- `color-focus-ring`: `#f2622e` – keyboard focus
